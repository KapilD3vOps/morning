Lead · UK · Business

Softer inflation tilts the Bank toward a November cut

July CPI came in at 2.4%, a tenth below consensus and the third consecutive downside surprise. Two dissenters on the MPC have shifted to the cut camp in overnight commentary, and rates markets now price a 62% chance of a 25bp move in November — up from 38% before the release.

Sterling weakened 0.2% against the dollar as the print landed; gilts caught a bid across the curve. FTSE futures indicate a modest opening rally led by rate-sensitives.

Powell speaks at Jackson Hole today — markets brace for a signal

The Fed chair's speech at 15:00 BST is his last major address before the September FOMC. Traders are pricing a 78% chance of a 25bp cut next month. A dovish tone would extend the equity rally; anything hawkish lands hard into thin summer volume.

Elizabeth line extension to Ebbsfleet clears preliminary Treasury review

A £2.1bn proposal to extend the Elizabeth line east through Dartford to Ebbsfleet cleared preliminary Treasury review yesterday. City Hall targets a 2029 construction start with services from 2035. Property analysts expect a repeat of the pre-opening price ripple seen along the original route.
